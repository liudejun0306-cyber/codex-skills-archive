# Printed-Form Will Package

This is one skill in the broader collection of Codex skills for Mainland Chinese legal practice.

It supports the preparation of structured document packages for wills made in printed form, including intake materials, property schedules, signing records, risk notices, witness-related documents, video scripts, and evidence indexes.

In this repository, **printed-form will** means a will prepared in printed form. It does not mean a printed product, a publication, or a commercial printing item.

The workflow keeps missing information as explicit placeholders, distinguishes drafting or consultation from witnessing, and requires the applicable statutes, judicial interpretations, local requirements, and actual signing process to be checked before professional use.
